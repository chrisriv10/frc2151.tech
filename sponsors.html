Thank You to Our Sponsors

We thank every sponsor, mentor, family, school partner, and community supporter who helps make FRC Team 2151 possible. Your support helps us buy robot parts, register for competitions, and give students hands-on experience with engineering, programming, design, marketing, and leadership.

The Monty Pythons are able to learn, compete, and represent Proviso Mathematics and Science Academy because people believe in our students. We are grateful for every donation, material contribution, and opportunity shared with the team.

Learn More

What We Accept as Contributions

No matter the size, every contribution helps our students grow. We welcome:

  • Monetary donations of any amount
  • Tools, materials, and robot parts
  • Food for build sessions
  • Mentorship and professional expertise
  • Support through team gear purchases in our team store

What Your Support Helps Fund

Your contributions go directly toward giving students hands-on experience in engineering, programming, design, marketing, and leadership:

  • Robot parts and manufacturing materials
  • Competition registration and travel
  • Tools and shop equipment
  • Student workshops and learning opportunities
  • Team outreach and community events

What Sponsors Receive

In return for your support, we offer:

  • Logo placement on the robot
  • Logo placement on team apparel
  • Recognition on our website and social media
  • Presence at competitions and community events
  • Connection to STEM-interested students
  • A specialized thank-you video from the team
  • Public acknowledgment at season's end

Interested in sponsoring FRC Team 2151? We would love to talk!
